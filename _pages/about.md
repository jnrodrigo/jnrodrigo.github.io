Hello! I am a master's student at the [University of the Philippines Diliman](https://upd.edu.ph),
advised by [Ian Vega](https://ianvega.wixsite.com/ianvega) at the [National Institute of Physics](https://nip.upd.edu.ph).
I am currently working on thermodynamic geometry with a view toward applications to exotic systems,
such as self-gravitating systems and black holes. More broadly, I am interested in gravitational physics.

I am also a junior faculty member at UP Diliman, where I teach introductory physics and oversee
lab classes for science and engineering students. A complete CV can be found [here](/assets/pdf/long-cv.pdf).

In my free time, I enjoy cooking and practicing yoga, and recently I made the expensive (but fun) mistake of getting
a scuba diving certification.
