I am currently thinking about thermodynamic geometry, which studies the various metrics that can be imposed on thermodynamic state space. Many foundational questions in this field remain unsettled -- for example, is there a preferred metric? I am particularly interested in applying these ideas to self-gravitating systems and black holes, which are known to be unusual from a thermodynamics perspective.
